About EdwardsApps

The software came last. The work came first.

EdwardsApps wasn't founded as a software company. It grew out of three working lives — a surfacing and groundworks contractor, a poverty-prevention charity and nine years behind the bar of a pub — and out of the tools Peter Edwards built because those jobs needed them.

The history

Three jobs before this one.

  • The surfacing firm

    Where it started

    Peter is a director of Edwards Surfacing, a surfacing and groundworks contractor. That's where the whole thing began: pricing jobs, scheduling crews, chasing invoices, wrestling CIS and Sage — and finding that no software on the market matched how the work actually ran. So he built his own. That tool became CrewBook, and it still runs the firm's scheduling, costing and invoicing every working day.

  • The charity

    Counting the tins

    As chairman of a poverty-prevention charity, Peter ran a real collection-pot campaign — pots out, cash back, and a paper trail the trustees had to stand behind. The record-keeping that came home with the tins is becoming Almoner, governance and fundraising software for charities.

  • The pub

    Nine years behind the bar

    Nine years as a pub landlord: cashing up the till, keeping the rotas, holding a small business together in public. Not a product — but it's where the habit of practical, no-nonsense tools was formed, and it's why everything EdwardsApps builds starts with how the work really runs on a bad week, not a good one.

Today

Software built from the inside.

EdwardsApps is those tools, grown up and offered to firms like the ones they came from. CrewBook for surfacing and groundworks contractors, OurSpace for households, Almoner for charities — and custom builds for the jobs that don't have a product yet. Edwards Surfacing is still trading at edwardssurfacing.co.uk, which means the flagship product is still tested the hard way: on real jobs, every working day.
