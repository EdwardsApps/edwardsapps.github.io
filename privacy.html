Privacy

Clear about the information we use.

This notice explains what reaches EdwardsApps when you use this website or the EdwardsApps Connect client app, or get in touch, why it is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Who is responsible

EdwardsApps is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. For privacy questions or requests, email peter@edwardsapps.co.uk.

Information we receive

  • Enquiries: your name, email address and anything you include when you email us.
  • Optional website analytics: information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type, browser and referral source, collected through Google Analytics only after you accept analytics.
  • Your consent choice: this website stores whether you accepted or declined analytics so it can respect that choice on later visits.

This website itself has no account area, checkout or contact form. Information held in our client app is covered in the EdwardsApps Connect section below. Product websites linked from EdwardsApps have their own privacy information.

Why we use it

  • To reply to enquiries, discuss a requested demo or decide whether a custom build is a good fit.
  • To understand, with consent, which pages are useful and improve the website.
  • To protect and operate the website and keep appropriate business records.

We rely on legitimate interests to answer general enquiries and operate the website, steps at your request before entering a contract where an enquiry concerns potential work, and consent for optional analytics. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time through Cookie settings.

Who processes information

Website and email service providers may process information to provide their services. If you accept analytics, Google processes analytics information for EdwardsApps. These providers may process information outside the UK under the safeguards that apply to their services.

How long we keep it

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as it is needed to handle the conversation, provide any requested work and meet reasonable business or legal record-keeping needs. Analytics retention is controlled within Google Analytics. Your browser keeps the consent choice until you change it or clear the site's stored data.

EdwardsApps Connect (client app)

EdwardsApps Connect is the client app for people we build and run apps for. It is available at connect.edwardsapps.co.uk and as an app for iPhone, iPad and Android. This part of the notice covers what Connect holds and why.

Information Connect holds

  • Your account: your email address and either a password or the sign-in you choose (Sign in with Apple or Google), plus your name, phone number and job title if you add them in Settings.
  • Your company's records: the company name and address, VAT and company registration numbers, and the names, email addresses and phone numbers of the people you tell us to contact about projects and invoices.
  • Your project with us: proposals we send you and whether you have viewed, accepted or declined them along with any note you add, service agreements, invoices and their payment status, the apps we have built for you, and documents we share with you such as agreements, data processing paperwork and guides.
  • Messages we send: a record of the emails Connect sends you, such as a new proposal or invoice, so we can see what you have received.

Anyone can create a Connect account, but it shows nothing until we link it to a client we work with. Connect does not use analytics or tracking, does not show advertising and does not send push notifications.

Why we use it

To provide the service you have engaged us for: sending and recording proposals, running your apps, invoicing and keeping in touch. This is performance of our contract with you. Invoices and agreements are also kept to meet our legal and tax record-keeping duties.

Who processes it

Connect is built on and hosted by Base44, which stores the data, handles sign-in and sends email on our behalf. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that provider confirms your identity to us. These providers may process information outside the UK under the safeguards that apply to their services. We do not sell or share client information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Your account and project records are kept while you are a client and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you return. Invoices, agreements and related records are kept for at least six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as UK law requires.

Closing your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings in Connect, which removes your login and profile immediately. Your company's business records are kept as above; to ask for those to be removed too, email peter@edwardsapps.co.uk from the address on your account. The full Connect policy is at connect.edwardsapps.co.uk/privacy.

Your choices and rights

You can ask about the personal information EdwardsApps holds about you and, where applicable, ask for it to be corrected, deleted, restricted or provided to you, or object to its use. Email peter@edwardsapps.co.uk to make a request.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your information has been handled.

Changes to this notice

This page will be updated when the website's or the Connect app's use of personal information changes. The latest revision date appears at the top.
