Cookies

Your choice, remembered.

The site needs one small piece of browser storage to remember your analytics choice. Google Analytics loads only if you accept it.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Essential preference storage

The site stores a value named ea-consent in your browser's local storage. It records whether you accepted or declined analytics so the banner does not ask on every page. It contains only the word granted or denied and is not used to identify you.

This preference remains until you change it, clear the site's stored data or use a browser setting that removes it.

Optional analytics

If you select “Accept analytics”, the site loads Google Analytics. Google Analytics may then use cookies or similar identifiers to measure visits, pages viewed, broad device information, referral sources and approximate location. EdwardsApps uses this information to understand which pages are useful and improve the site.

If you decline, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. Advertising storage, personalisation and user-data consent remain denied on this website.

Change your choice

Open Cookie settings at any time. Your new choice takes effect immediately for future activity.
