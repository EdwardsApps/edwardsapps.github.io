The right view for the job

One operation.
Three perspectives.

The first release focuses on the handover between the people doing the work, the team coordinating it and the client who needs to know what happened.

01 / ON SITE

For officers

See assigned shifts and site details, check in and capture patrol findings or incidents. Submit a report for review and respond when more detail is needed.

  • Shift assignments and site information
  • Check-ins and shift completion
  • Reports and incident records
  • Photos and videos in the phone app, stamped with time and place
02 / IN CONTROL

For the control room

Coordinate sites and shifts, assign officers and follow the work through. Review incoming reports, return them for changes or release an approved version to the client.

  • Site and shift coordination
  • Officer assignments
  • Report review and release
03 / KEPT INFORMED

For clients

A dedicated workspace for the information shared with them. Read released reports and raise requests, with access tied to the client’s own sites and records.

  • Approved client reports
  • A clear record of shared information
  • Requests and follow-up

From assignment to approved report

A clear handover. At every step.

  1. 01

    Assign

    Set up the site, plan the shift and give your officer the details.

  2. 02

    Record

    Check in and capture patrol findings or incidents as they happen.

  3. 03

    Review

    Check the report and prepare the version your client should see.

  4. 04

    Share

    Release it to the client’s workspace and handle follow-up requests.

Who is SecuriBeacon for?

Security companies coordinating officers, sites, shifts and client reporting.

Can I download it now?

The web workspace is ready now at securibeacon.base44.app. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps are coming soon to the App Store and Google Play, and use the same account.

What will it cost?

£29 per company per month, including VAT. One subscription covers every officer, controller and client in your company. Subscribe on the SecuriBeacon website or in the iPhone app; it renews monthly until you cancel.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. In the app, tap the settings cog; on the website, open Settings. Download your information, then delete your account. We complete deletion within one month. See the SecuriBeacon privacy notice.
