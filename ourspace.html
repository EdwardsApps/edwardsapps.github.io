OurSpace

One household. One app.

OurSpace keeps a household's moving parts in one place — shared tasks, calendars, budgets, lists and reminders — so the mental load is shared too. It's live on the Apple App Store.

Download on the App Store Visit our-space.me

What it does

The household admin, handled together.

Shared to-do lists that both of you can see and tick off. A calendar that shows the whole household's week, not just one person's. Budgets and money overviews you can talk about over the same screen. Reminders that fire when they're useful — including when you're near the shop, not after you've left it.

OurSpace has its own home on the web — the full feature tour, guides and support all live there.
