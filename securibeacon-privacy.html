SecuriBeacon · Privacy

Your information in SecuriBeacon.

This notice explains what SecuriBeacon holds about officers, control room staff, clients and company administrators, why, who can see it, and how to download or delete it. It covers the SecuriBeacon website at securibeacon.base44.app and the SecuriBeacon apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

SecuriBeacon is used by security companies to run their work. For your SecuriBeacon account, sign-in and a company's subscription, EdwardsApps is the data controller. For the operational records a company keeps in SecuriBeacon (its sites, shifts, reports and requests), the security company you work for or buy from is the controller and EdwardsApps processes them on its behalf. Questions about those records usually go to that company first, but you are always welcome to contact us.

Information we hold

  • Your account: your name, email address and password, or the confirmation Apple or Google gives us if you choose to sign in with them. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only see the relay address.
  • Company access: which companies you belong to, your role (for example officer, controller or client), your display name and whether your access is active.
  • Work records: sites and their instructions, the shifts assigned to you, when you checked in and finished, the reports you write, the corrections asked for and the client requests and replies you are involved in.
  • Location: described in the next section.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, a device token from Apple or Google so we can send alerts to your phone, the workspaces you chose, and a record of which notifications were sent.
  • Billing: for company administrators who subscribe, the company's subscription status and payment dates. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.
  • Account requests: if you ask for your account to be deleted, a record of that request and its progress.

SecuriBeacon does not show advertising, does not sell information and does not use your information to build marketing profiles. The phone apps contain no third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Location

SecuriBeacon only uses your location when you ask it to, and only while you are on a shift.

  • Checking in and finishing a shift: when you capture your location, SecuriBeacon records your position, its accuracy and how far you were from the site, as evidence for that attendance record. If location is not available you can give a reason instead.
  • Sharing your duty location: this is optional and off until you start it. While it is on, your current position and its accuracy are sent to your company's control room. Only the latest position is kept, it is hidden after 90 seconds without an update, and it is cleared when you stop sharing, change workspace, sign out or finish the shift. On iPhone you can choose to keep sharing with the screen locked, and your phone then shows that location is in use. On Android, sharing stops when you leave the app. Clients never see an officer's location.

You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings.

Who can see it

Everything in SecuriBeacon belongs to a company workspace, and each company only sees its own. Within a company, people see what their role allows: control room staff see the company's sites, shifts, reports and live duty locations; officers see their own shifts and reports; clients see only their own sites, visits and the reports the control room has chosen to release to them. Lock-screen notifications say that there is an update but carry no operational details.

Why we use it

  • To provide SecuriBeacon to your company and to you, which is the performance of our contract with the company and, for your account, with you.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent misuse and fix problems, which is our legitimate interest in running a safe service.
  • To keep billing and tax records, which is a legal obligation.
  • Location sharing and push notifications are used only when you turn them on.

Who processes information

SecuriBeacon is built on and hosted by Base44, which stores the data, handles sign-in and sends account emails. Apple and Google confirm your identity if you sign in with them and deliver push notifications to your phone. Stripe processes company subscription payments. On iPhone and iPad the control room map uses Apple Maps. These providers may process information outside the UK under the safeguards that apply to their services. We do not share information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your account is kept while you use SecuriBeacon. A company's work records are kept while the company uses SecuriBeacon, so that it has a reliable record of its shifts, reports and requests, and are removed when the company closes its account, apart from anything we must keep by law. Live duty locations are cleared as described above. Billing records are kept for six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as UK law requires. Our hosting provider may keep backup copies for a limited period before they are overwritten.

Download and delete

In the SecuriBeacon app, tap the settings cog at the top of the screen; on the website, open Settings. From there you can:

  • Download your information as a file containing your profile, your company access and the records linked to you.
  • Delete your account. After you have downloaded your information, confirm the request. We delete your account and the personal information linked to it, including your shifts, location records and reports, within one month and email you when it is done.

You can also ask by emailing peter@edwardsapps.co.uk from the address on your account.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected, deleted, restricted or given to you, or object to its use. Email peter@edwardsapps.co.uk. Where your company is the controller of a record, we will work with it to answer your request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

SecuriBeacon is a working tool for adults employed by or contracted to security companies and their clients. It is not intended for children.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when SecuriBeacon's use of personal information changes. The latest revision date appears at the top. The EdwardsApps website itself is covered by our main privacy notice.
