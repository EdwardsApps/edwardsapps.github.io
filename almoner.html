Almoner — coming

Every pot accounted for.

Almoner is collection-pot, fundraising and governance software for charities. It grew out of running a real registered charity's collection-pot campaign — and out of the paperwork that came home with the pots.

Register interest

The problem

Cash in tins, records on paper.

Collection pots go out, cash comes back, and in between sits a paper trail the trustees have to stand behind. Most small charities track pots, counts and banking in notebooks and spreadsheets — honest work that's hard to evidence, and harder still to hand over when a volunteer moves on.

What's coming

Stewardship you can show.

  • Pot tracking — where every pot is, who placed it, and when it's due for collection.
  • Counted-and-banked records with a clear trail from tin to bank statement.
  • Campaign totals the fundraising team and the trustees read the same way.
  • Governance reporting that turns good practice into evidence.

Almoner is early, and it's being shaped by people who count the tins. If you help run a charity and this sounds like yours, register your interest and tell us how your collections actually work.
