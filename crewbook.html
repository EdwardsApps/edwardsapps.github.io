CrewBook

The job book your whole firm runs on.

Job management for surfacing and groundworks contractors — scheduling, costing, invoicing and CIS in one place. CrewBook was built inside a working contractor, and it runs that firm today.

Request a demo Visit crewbook.me

The problem

You know this mess.

Jobs on a whiteboard. Crews organised through WhatsApp threads. Day rates and materials scribbled on the back of delivery notes. Invoices built by hand in a spreadsheet, and CIS worked out at the kitchen table on the last weekend of the month. It works — right up until two jobs move, one crew splits, and nobody tells the office.

CrewBook replaces that patchwork with one place where the schedule, the costs and the invoices all agree with each other.

What it does

One place. One version of the truth.

  • Scheduling and job tracking

    See every job, every crew, every day. Move a job and the whole picture moves with it — no rubbing out the whiteboard, no ringing round to check who knows.

  • Labour, materials and plant

    Capture day rates, materials and plant against the job as it happens — not from memory a fortnight later. Every job shows what it actually cost, while there's still time to do something about it.

  • Invoicing, including proforma

    Build invoices and proformas straight from the job. The numbers come from what was captured on site, not re-typed from a pile of paper — so the invoice matches the work.

  • CIS-aware Sage 50 export

    CrewBook routes every invoice line the way CIS expects — labour netted for deduction and materials paid gross, gross-status lump sums left untouched, and non-CIS work left alone — then exports it clean to Sage 50. No re-keying, no month-end untangling.

  • WhatsApp job notifications

    Crews get the job where they already look: WhatsApp. Where, when and what's needed — sent from the schedule, not typed out by the office each evening.

Where it comes from

Born on site, not in a pitch deck.

CrewBook wasn't designed from interviews with contractors. It was built inside one — a working surfacing and groundworks firm — and it has run that firm's scheduling, costing and invoicing every working day since. When something didn't survive contact with a real Monday morning, it got fixed, because the builder was also the user.
